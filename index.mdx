Sahara is an anonymous peer-support platform built on Next.js, Supabase, and LiveKit. Users join topic-based group sessions, speak freely under full identity protection, and receive AI-generated wellbeing trend insights drawn from their session history and private journal — with a direct escalation path to verified counselors when needed.

## How Sahara Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join or create a session">
    Users browse topic-based support groups and join a live video or text session. Three session types are supported: peer-only, peer + counselor, and one-on-one with a counselor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk freely — audio is captured">
    LiveKit handles real-time video and per-participant audio recording. Identity is anonymized throughout; no personally identifiable information is exposed to other participants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-session transcription">
    After the session ends, audio recordings are automatically transcribed using Groq Whisper (`whisper-large-v3`). Transcripts are stored privately under the participant's account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI wellbeing analysis">
    An LLM analyses the transcript alongside the user's previous session history and opted-in journal entries, producing a summary, trend (`improving` / `stable` / `declining`), and key themes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
